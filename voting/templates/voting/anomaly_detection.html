🎓 Technical Explanation (For Viva)

What is Anomaly Detection?

Anomaly detection identifies unusual patterns that don't conform to expected behavior. In voting systems, this helps detect fraud, system errors, or suspicious activities.

Algorithm Used: Z-Score Method

Formula: Z = (X - μ) / σ

  • X: Actual votes in an hour
  • μ (mu): Mean (average) votes per hour
  • σ (sigma): Standard deviation

Why This Matters:

  • Election Security: Detect vote manipulation attempts
  • Fraud Prevention: Identify suspicious voting patterns
  • System Integrity: Ensure fair and transparent elections
  • Real-time Alerts: Immediate notification of irregularities
